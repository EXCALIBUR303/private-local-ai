Reach the internet for watchers

Off. Nothing in this app makes an outbound request. The model, your documents and your conversations stay local either way — this switch only governs status polls to the services below.

It keeps looking while you do not. Each watch runs on its own schedule and only speaks up when something changed — a new mail, a deployment that went red, a URL that stopped answering. Mail is read from Mail.app on this Mac. Everything a watch collects is treated as data to report, never as instructions to follow.
