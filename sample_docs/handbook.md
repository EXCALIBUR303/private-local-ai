# Northwind Handbook

Northwind is a small hardware distributor operating from Osaka and Rotterdam.

## Support hours

Support runs 09:00–17:00 JST on weekdays. Out-of-hours pages go to the
on-call engineer via the internal rota. There is no weekend coverage.

## Return policy

Customers may return unopened stock within 30 days for a full refund.
Opened stock carries a 15% restocking fee. Custom orders are non-returnable.

## Escalation

Any incident lasting more than four hours is escalated to the operations
lead, currently Priya Raman. Incidents affecting more than 50 customers are
escalated immediately, regardless of duration.
